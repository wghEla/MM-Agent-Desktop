## Final source finding and fix

### RC-F1 — S0 producer contract was enforced too late

Prior behavior:

- Reader instructions only said to produce `交接/题面契约.json` and
  `交接/数据档案.json`;
- the Reader's `ExpectedArtifact` entries did not bind their Pydantic schemas;
- the first real-provider gate observed semantic-key drift such as English/synonym keys
  (`题目` instead of canonical `赛题`);
- malformed/noncanonical output could therefore survive the Reader artifact acceptance and
  only fail later at G0.

This was not a reason to weaken G0.  The producer contract was simply too implicit.

Source fix:

- `ProblemContract` and `DataArchive` are now attached directly to the Reader
  `ExpectedArtifact` definitions;
- the Reader instruction receives the Runtime-generated
  `model_json_schema()` projection of those same Pydantic models;
- the prompt explicitly says not to translate canonical Chinese contract fields into
  English/synonyms and to write pure JSON without Markdown fences;
- no second hand-maintained schema copy is introduced;
- G0 behavior is unchanged and remains fail-closed.

Distinguishing test:

`tests/pipeline/test_s0_s1.py::test_s0_reader_enforces_canonical_contracts_before_g0`

It writes a contract using `题目` instead of `赛题` and proves the Reader artifact
acceptance raises `ArtifactInvalid` before G0.
